Reorder calculation

  • Included statuses: Live, Fashion and Backorder.
  • Excluded statuses: Feeds Only, Internal Use and Presentation.
  • Only brands marked Active are included.
  • Recommended Reorder Qty uses average monthly demand × (brand lead time + configured coverage months), plus configured critical/minimum carrying units and client orders, less on-hand stock and eligible supplier units arriving within the configured supplier window. Results at or below zero are set to zero and positive quantities are rounded up.

ABC rules

Items are ranked by Vol Past 3M. Class A covers the first 80% of cumulative sales units, Class B covers 80%–95%, and Class C contains the remaining items. Thresholds can be changed on the Dashboard.

Expected source columns

Brand, itemid, Model#, Item Title, Status, ETA, Vol Past 3M, Vol Last 30 Days, Avg/PerM Past 3M, Stock Qty, Stock Available, STOCK difference, OPEN ORDERS FROM CLIENT and OPEN ORDERS TO SUPPLIER.
