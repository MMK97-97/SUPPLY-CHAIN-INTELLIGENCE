Supply Chain Intelligence · Enterprise Operating Manual

Official system handbook, operational workflows, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).

01 Executive Platform Architecture

Supply Chain Intelligence (SCI) is a high-availability, unified enterprise execution platform designed for multi-region inventory forecasting, omnichannel order routing, vendor procurement, warehouse management (WMS), and distributed 3PL logistics.

Core Architecture Guarantees

  • Single Source of Truth: One connected state graph connects SKUs, customer orders, allocations, vendor POs, warehouse bins, and 3PL routing across all modules.
  • Atomic Operations & Guardrails: Inventory mutations (reservations, receiving, pick wave locking, bin transfers, packing, and dispatch) execute with strict transactional invariants to prevent overselling, negative balances, or duplicate fulfillment.
  • Offline-First Browser Execution: The platform operates with local in-browser persistence (LocalStorage, IndexedDB, SessionStorage) guaranteeing continuous operations during network interruptions.
  • Seamless View Transitions: Synchronized @view-transition cross-fades and active session tracking eliminate full-screen loading splash screens during in-app navigation.

03 Enterprise Control Tower (workspace.html)

The Control Tower serves as the unified executive cockpit across all operational groups:

  • Operational Modules Grid: Fast-launch command cards for Item Master, Orders, Procurement, WMS, Shipping, 3PL, Transportation, and Analytics. Each card features real-time active indicators and direct shortcuts (e.g. + Create order, + Create vendor PO).
  • Telemetry & KPIs View: Real-time operations metrics displaying Total Orders, On-Time Delivery Rate (%), Available Inventory, Digital Licenses Allocated, Open Vendor Commitments, and Regional Credit Exposure.
  • Demand Trend & Network Topology: Live orders vs. dispatches trend chart and regional logistics node visualization.

04 Master Item Catalog & ATS Feeds (item-master.html)

Maintains the multi-region commercial catalog with real-time Available-to-Sell (ATS) stock computation:

Available-to-Sell (ATS) Formula

ATS Stock = Physical On-Hand Stock - Active Customer Reservations

  • Catalog Filter & Search: Filter across Live, Fashion, Backorder, Internal Use, and Feeds Only statuses. Status pills indicate In Stock, Low Stock, and Out of Stock.
  • Add New Catalog Item (add-item.html): Form to register new SKUs with unique model numbers, brands, categories, costs, wholesale prices, and default warehouse bin slotting.
  • ATS Feed Upload: Ingest supplier-provided available-to-sell spreadsheets to update vendor availability in bulk without manual entry.

05 Omnichannel Order Management (all-open-orders.html)

Controls customer commitments from initial order capture to validation, picking, and dispatch.

  • Order Creation (create-order.html): Select customer account, input PO number, select line items, and choose fulfillment channel (WAREHOUSE, 3PL, DROPSHIP). Evaluates available credit limits before order confirmation.
  • Non-Destructive Allocation: Orders immediately reserve stock to prevent overselling. Physical on-hand inventory is not deducted until actual warehouse dispatch.
  • All Open Orders (all-open-orders.html): Complete operational table of active orders with line item drawer, packing slip printing, and real-time status pills.
  • Exception Queues: Specialized pipelines for order-management/issues.html (credit holds, address failures), unvalidated.html, cancelled.html, and hold-redemption.html.

06 Vendor & Supplier Management (vendor-management/)

Manages supplier records, contractual compliance, and performance ratings:

  • Vendor Master (vendors.html): Registry of active suppliers, remit-to addresses, payment terms, and authorized product categories.
  • SLA Scorecard (sla.html): Tracks On-Time In-Full (OTIF) rates, vendor defect ratios from dock inspections, and contracted lead time variance.
  • Digital Vault (digital-vault.html): Custody log for digital software keys and tokenized partner rewards.

07 Procurement & Vendor PO Lifecycle (procurement/)

Directs the inbound replenishment pipeline from PO generation to tracking and dock receipt:

  • Vendor PO Management (vendor-po-management.html): Master table of all open supplier POs, ordered vs. shipped quantities, carrier tracking, and dynamic shipping windows with overdue alerts.
  • Interactive PO Details Drawer: Click any PO number to view line models, vendor part numbers, unit costs, and manage uploaded packing slips (PDF, PNG, JPG).
  • Creating a Vendor PO (create-vendor-po.html): Wizard to select vendor, receiving warehouse, delivery window, and multiple catalog models.
  • Dropship PO Management (dropship-po.html): Directly links customer orders to manufacturer dropship POs. When vendor dispatch is confirmed with tracking, the customer order updates to DISPATCHED without touching warehouse inventory.

08 Warehouse Management System (WMS) (warehouse-management/)

Governs physical dock receiving, bin storage, wave picking, packing benches, and cycle counts:

  • Receiving & Putaway (receiving.html): Reconciles inbound deliveries against open Vendor POs. Damaged units are quarantined from ATS sellable stock. Automatically updates PO status idempotently.
  • Inventory & Bins (inventory-bins.html): Aisle-bay-shelf-bin location mapping. Supports internal bin-to-bin transfers while strictly protecting units reserved for active pick waves.
  • Wave Picking (wave-picking.html): Groups customer orders into optimized picking waves sequenced by shortest walking path.
  • Packing & Dispatch (packing-dispatch.html): Verifies cartons, generates tracking numbers, and executes the final atomic on-hand stock deduction upon carrier handover.
  • Cycle Counts (cycle-counts.html): Periodic blind inventory counts and variance reconciliation audits.

09 Distributed 3PL Logistics (3pl-management/)

Orchestrates remote third-party logistics fulfillment:

  • Partner Routing Rules: Automatically directs customer orders to 3PL partners based on proximity or regional distribution rules.
  • Outbound API Contract: Formatted to stark.3pl.order.v1 with deterministic idempotency keys to eliminate duplicate dispatches.
  • Batch Confirmation Upload (upload-shipments.html): Ingests partner CSV/Excel shipment tracking confirmations and reconciles open customer orders in bulk.

10 Shipping, Freight & Transportation (shipping/)

Centralizes carrier pickups, freight rate estimation, and tracking:

  • Sarasota Hub Operations (shipping/index.html): Active queue for internal Sarasota packing and carrier handover.
  • Daily Shipment Report (shipping/daily-report.html): Chronological record of all carrier dispatches, gross weight, and Bills of Lading.
  • Freight Estimator & Consolidator (freight-estimator.html): Calculates LTL/FTL freight rates across NMFC classes and optimizes multi-order pallet consolidation.
  • Multi-Carrier Tracking (shipment-tracking.html): Real-time lookup for FedEx, UPS, DHL, and Freight tracking numbers.

11 Replenishment Intelligence (inventory-dashboard-us.html)

Predictive regional replenishment and inventory optimization:

Enterprise Replenishment Formula

Reorder Qty = ⌈ Demand × (Lead Time + Coverage) + Safety Stock + Client Orders - On Hand - Inbound POs ⌉

14 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

SOP 1: Catalog Creation & Initial Stock Putaway

1
Add Item: Open add-item.html, enter SKU, Model, Brand, Category, Cost, and Wholesale Price. Click Save.
2
Issue Vendor PO: Open create-vendor-po.html, select supplier, target warehouse, delivery window, and add item line. Submit PO.
3
Receive at Dock: Open receiving.html, enter PO#, verify count, designate warehouse bin, and click Confirm Putaway. Stock is now sellable.

SOP 2: Fulfilling Customer Orders

1
Create Order: Open create-order.html, select customer account, PO#, and line items. Stock is reserved automatically.
2
Pick Wave: Open wave-picking.html, generate pick wave, and execute physical item retrieval.
3
Pack & Dispatch: Open packing-dispatch.html, cartonize items, enter carrier tracking, and click Dispatch Order. Physical stock is deducted.

Purchase OrderDRAFT, ISSUED, ACKNOWLEDGED, SHIPPED, RECEIVED, CANCELLEDReceipt updates PO balance and increments physical warehouse stock. Idempotent execution.
Dropship POISSUED, DISPATCHEDDirect supplier dispatch updates customer order to DISPATCHED with zero warehouse receipt or stock deduction.

16 Disaster Recovery & Cloud Synchronization

Local Backup Snapshot: Navigate to data-center.html $\rightarrow$ Click Export System Backup to save a complete, offline JSON snapshot.

Cloud Concurrency: Supabase cloud synchronization uses optimistic revision locks (recordRevision). In the event of a concurrent edit from another workstation, the sync pauses safely and prompts reconciliation rather than overwriting newer data.
