Receiving inspection

Create a prefilled inspection form for your 3PL to complete and return after delivery.

Files are processed in this browser. Download a receiving packet before closing the tab to keep the record and originals.

Blank PDF Blank Word
1

Source documents

Upload the vendor PO and packing slip, then extract and review the model quantities.

PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text and JPG, PNG or WebP. Up to 20 MB per file. Scanned pages use English OCR.

2

Shipment details

Confirm the PO, shipping method, and shipment reference before sending the form.

3

Review line items

Check model, ordered quantity, and packing slip quantity. Receiving fields in the 3PL template will be blank.

4

Send to your 3PL

Download the prefilled form and email it to your warehouse. Ask them to return it with the signed BOL or POD and the arrival and model photos. Word lets them insert pictures directly; with PDF they can return pictures as labeled attachments.

5

Record returned inspection

Optional: record the received counts above and attach the evidence here to keep a complete receiving packet.

You can attach multiple PDFs and images.

Required if the pallet was damaged or tampered with.

Required if damage or tampering is reported.

6

Download receiving record

Shortage is calculated as packing slip quantity minus received quantity, with a minimum of zero. The packet keeps both reports and the original files.
